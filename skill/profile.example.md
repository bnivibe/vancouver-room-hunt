# Room-hunt profile (copy to profile.md, keep it private)

The skill reads `profile.md` before every search. Everything here is the
renter's to decide; the skill only applies it. Date every change, because
criteria move during a search and yesterday's filter applied as if it were
today's wastes a day.

## Anchors

- Main anchor (where most weekdays are spent): e.g. a library, a campus, an office
- Second anchor, if any, and how often it is used
- Walking limit that counts as "walkable": 35 minutes

## Dates and term

- Move-in date: YYYY-MM-DD (is a later date acceptable? yes/no)
- Minimum and maximum term: e.g. month-to-month up to 6 months, no 1-year lease
- When the job location becomes known, which changes the anchor

## Knockouts (facts only)

- Real bedroom with a bed and a window
- At most N people in the unit, one per bedroom
- Nobody sleeping in the living room, den or solarium
- No basement (yes/no)
- Written agreement required (yes/no)
- Gates that cannot be passed: credit check, employment letter
- Household: children / dogs / couples acceptable? cats?
- Kitchen with a stove, and cooking that smells (say what you cook)

## Ranking (in order)

1. e.g. soundproofing grade
2. e.g. fewer people, private bathroom, no shared bedroom wall
3. e.g. location band (downtown peninsula, City of Vancouver, suburbs)
4. monthly total

## Money

- Budget file for `scripts/score.py --budget`: path, and the date it was last updated
- Ceiling, if any: a monthly total (rent + utilities + transit), not a rent figure
- Baseline room to compare against

## Delivery and records

- Phone channel for every finished search (a chat webhook; name the environment variable, never the URL)
- Where the living ledger, the reports and the listing archive are kept
- Calendar for viewings

## What the renter does, never the scout

Joining groups, posting, messaging landlords, booking viewings, signing, paying.
